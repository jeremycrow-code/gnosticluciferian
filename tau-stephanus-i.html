THE MOST REV. DR. STEPHAN A. HOELLER (TAU STEPHANUS I) // NOVEMBER 27, 1931 – MAY 3, 2026 // IN MEMORIAM

The Most Rev. Dr. Stephan A. Hoeller (Tau Stephanus I) (November 27, 1931 – May 3, 2026) was an internationally revered esoteric scholar, author, and Presiding Bishop of the Ecclesia Gnostica. Born in Budapest, Hungary, he carried the deep cultural and intellectual heritage of Central Europe before emigrating to the United States, where he became one of the foremost architects of the 20th and 21st century Gnostic revival.

For over sixty years, Bishop Hoeller directed the Gnostic Society in Los Angeles and served for decades on the lecturing faculty of Manly P. Hall's Philosophical Research Society (PRS). Through canonical treatises such as Gnosticism: New Light on the Ancient Tradition of Inner Knowing, Jung and the Lost Gospels, and The Gnostic Jung and the Seven Sermons to the Dead, he rescued ancient Gnosis from dry historical polemics, presenting it as an active, living psychological and transcendental path of awakening.

"The Gnostic lives in a world that is not his true home, yet he is called not to curse the darkness, but to kindle within it the remembrance of the Light."
Dr. Stephan A. Hoeller, Jung and the Lost Gospels (1989)

Ecclesiastical Provenance & Offices

  • Sacerdotal Ordination (1958): Ordained to the Holy Priesthood of the American Catholic Church by Bishop Lowell Paul Wadle.
  • Episcopal Consecration (April 9, 1967): Consecrated to the Holy Episcopate by Richard, Duc de Palatine (Ronald Powell), Patriarch of the Pre-Nicene Gnostic Catholic Church and Head of the Sanctuary of the Gnosis in London, formally vesting him with the English Gnostic Transmission as Regionary Bishop for the Americas.
  • Primacy of the Ecclesia Gnostica: Guided the oldest continuously operating Gnostic ecclesiastical body in the United States, maintaining a weekly public Divine Liturgy and theurgic lectures until his entry into the Pleroma on May 3, 2026, at the age of 94.
  • The 1991 College of Métropolitains Concordat: Participated directly alongside Primate +Robert M. Cokinis (EGA) and Archbishop +Ronald V. Cappello (Old Templar Church) in the historic 1991 ecumenical synod, formally normalizing and cross-communicating his English Old Catholic, Liberal Catholic, and Syrian-Antiochene successions with Bishop +Carl Emmanuel (Tau Cerinthus II) of the Canadian See.

The English Gnostic & Sanctuary of the Gnosis Line

The tactile succession conveyed by Bishop Hoeller represents a unique synthesis of 19th-century English theosophical and sacramental currents. Originating through the Old Catholic Church of Utrecht, this stream was infused with the mystical and initiatory focus of the early Liberal Catholic movement through Charles Webster Leadbeater and James Ingall Wedgwood, before being reconstituted into an explicitly Pre-Nicene, Gnostic framework under Hugh George de Willmott Newman (Mar Georgius) and Richard, Duc de Palatine:

Associated Antiochian & Vilatte Succession

In addition to the Old Catholic succession of Utrecht, the consecration of Richard, Duc de Palatine by Mar Georgius conveyed the historic Syrian Patriarchate of Antioch (Syro-Antiochene Vilatte Line), descending through Archbishop Joseph René Vilatte (1892). While detailed in its exhaustive succession on our dedicated Syrian Patriarchate of Antioch Registry, this current entered the Canadian See of Tau Cerinthus II concurrently through Bishop Hoeller's 1991 synodal normalization.

Scholarly & Liturgical Legacy

Dr. Hoeller's lifelong dedication to intellectual rigor, psychological depth, and non-dogmatic liturgical continuity established the Ecclesia Gnostica as a sanctuary for modern spiritual seekers. His synthesis of C.G. Jung's analytical psychology with classical Valentinian cosmology bridged historical scholarship and lived ritual practice.

Through the reciprocal recognitions of the 1991 College of Métropolitains Concordat, Bishop Hoeller's English Old Catholic and Pre-Nicene transmissions were formally shared with Bishop +Carl Emmanuel, ensuring that these British and Syrian lines remain an integral part of the living Canadian See of Cerinthus.
