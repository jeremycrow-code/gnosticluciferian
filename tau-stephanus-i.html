THE MOST REV. DR. STEPHAN A. HOELLER (TAU STEPHANUS I) // REGIONARY BISHOP OF THE ECCLESIA GNOSTICA

The Most Rev. Dr. Stephan A. Hoeller (Tau Stephanus I) is widely recognized as one of the preeminent pioneering scholars, authors, and senior bishops of the modern Gnostic revival in North America. Serving for decades as the Presiding and Regionary Bishop of the Ecclesia Gnostica in Los Angeles, California, and Director of Studies for the Gnostic Society, his scholarly monographs, including Gnosticism: New Light on the Ancient Tradition of Inner Knowing and Jung and the Lost Gospels, helped recover classical Christian and Valentinian Gnosis from the footnotes of heresiology and reintroduce it into contemporary philosophical discourse.

Beyond his literary and academic contributions, Bishop Hoeller is the primary historical vehicle for what is termed the English Gnostic Transmission in the Western Hemisphere, holding valid tactile apostolic lines descending from the Old Catholic and English independent theurgic currents of Bishop Richard, Duc de Palatine (Ronald Powell).
