Igreja Católica Apostólica Brasileira (ICAB, Brazilian Line): Derived from Dom Carlos Duarte Costa (former Roman Catholic Bishop of Botucatu, founder of ICAB in 1945), descending through Dom Antidio Vargas to Patriarch Pedro Freire (Mar Petrus Johannes XIII, consecrated December 27, 1970). Hoeller (Tau Stephanus I), and imparted to the Canadian See during the 1991 Concordat. Detailed on the Tau Stephanus I Dossier.

The Landmark North American Concordats

The Constitution of Synesius (Seine Synod, 1906)

Established under Patriarch Léonce Fabre des Essarts (Tau Synésius), defining the constitutional authority of the Gnostic College of Metropolitans as the supreme collegiate governing body of the Church, establishing autocephalous metropolitan sees operating in autonomous fraternal parity rather than monarchical submission.

The 1991 Ecumenical Gnostic College of Metropolitans Concordat

Convened under the constitutional guidance of French Patriarch René Chambellant (Tau Renatus) and coordinated by The Most Rev. +Robert M. Cokinis (Tau Charles Harmonius II), Primate of North America for the Église Gnostique Apostolique. This accord formally united:

  • The Most Rev. +Carl Emmanuel (Tau Cerinthus II): Metropolitan Bishop of Canada, Gnostic Church of the Glorious Christ.
  • The Most Rev. +Stephan A. Hoeller (Tau Stephanus I): Regionary Bishop, Ecclesia Gnostica.
  • The Most Rev. +Ronald V. Cappello (Tau Mikaël I): Archbishop & Primate, The Old Templar Church.

The Synod of 2000 (Chapelle de Saint Montsalvat)

Convened on November 11, 2000, in Villa Park, Illinois. Bishop +Carl Emmanuel joined leading North American hierarchs in co-celebrating the Pontifical Liturgy, formally constituting the Saint Collège Électoral of the Église Gnostique Catholique Apostolica (Synode des États-Unis d'Amérique du Nord), uniting French Neo-Gnostic, Brazilian Catholic, and Chaldean lines.

The Ecumenical Concelebration of April 6, 2002

A major synodal consecration (sub conditione) uniting Bishop +Carl Emmanuel (Tau Cerinthus II), Patriarch Ronald V. Cappello (Tau Mikaël), Bishop Alberto I. LaCava (Tau Ignatius of Alexandria VII), Bishop Osmel Valera d'Abela, and Bishop Phillip Andrew Garver (Tau Vincent II). This accord cross-communicated fresh Italian Gnostic successions and Thelemic E.G.C. lines into the Canadian See ten months prior to the ordination of Jeremy Crow.
