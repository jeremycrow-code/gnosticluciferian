Tau Ogdoade-Orfeo IV

SOVEREIGN PATRIARCH MICHAEL BERTIAUX // PRIMATE OF THE ECCLESIA GNOSTICA SPIRITUALIS & HIEROPHANT OF THE FRANCO-HAITIAN CURRENT

The Most Rev. Michael Bertiaux (Tau Ogdoade-Orfeo IV) (born January 18, 1935) is an American occult philosopher, theurgist, artist, and independent Gnostic prelate whose visionary corpus expanded modern esoteric cosmology. Based for decades in Chicago, Illinois, Bertiaux is internationally known as the architect of the Voudon-Gnostic current and author of The Voudon Gnostic Workbook (1988).

Serving as Sovereign Patriarch of the Ecclesia Gnostica Spiritualis, Sovereign Grand Conservator of the Rite of Memphis-Misraïm, and Hierophant of the Fraternitas Ordo Hermeticus (incorporating the Choronzon Club, O.T.O.A., and La Couleuvre Noire sanctuaries), Bertiaux united classical French Neo-Gnosticism, high-grade Egyptian Masonry, Martinism, and Franco-Haitian esoteric metaphysics into an operative discipline of consciousness.

"Gnosis is the direct realization of cosmic consciousness. It is not merely a philosophical theory, but an experiential entry into the inner spaces of being, where the soul awakens to its own transcendent sovereignty."
Michael Bertiaux, The Voudon Gnostic Workbook

Philosophical Formation & The Haitian Consecration

Bertiaux's foundational background was academic and theological. Educated under Jesuit fathers at Seattle University, with graduate studies in philosophy and theology at Tulane and the University of British Columbia, his writing reflects the phenomenology of Edmund Husserl, Ernst Cassirer, Ludwig Wittgenstein, and G.W.F. Hegel, alongside analytical psychology.

  • Anglican Ministry & Academic Commission (Early 1960s): Graduating from an Anglican seminary, Bertiaux served as curate of an Episcopal parish in West Seattle before receiving a commission to teach philosophy at the Anglican Church College in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, preparing under the anthropologist Margaret Mead.
  • The Haitian Consecration (August 15, 1963): While in Port-au-Prince, Bertiaux entered the esoteric circles surrounding Hector-François Jean-Maine (Tau Ogdoade-Orfeo II). Initiated into the internal theurgical rites of La Couleuvre Noire (The Black Snake), Bertiaux was consecrated to the episcopate within the Neo-Pythagorean Gnostic Church, succeeding as Tau Ogdoade-Orfeo IV.
  • The Theosophical & Chicago Milieu (1964–1970s): Relocating to Illinois, Bertiaux worked for the Theosophical Society in America at Wheaton, collaborating with Liberal Catholic and independent bishops including Dr. Stephan A. Hoeller and Bishop Gregory of the Russian Orthodox tradition. In Chicago, while serving professionally as a state social work supervisor, he developed the Monastery of the Seven Rays as an operative esoteric curriculum.
  • Theurgical Physics & Esoteric Art: Bertiaux integrated esoteric philosophy with visual art, radionics, and what he designated trans-yuggothian transmission stations, treating creative works as operative instruments and doorways into the unconscious.

The Neo-Luciferian Church Consecrations (July 3, 2001)

Throughout his writings, Michael Bertiaux presented the Luciferian current not through vulgar sensationalism, but as a therapeutic and Promethean catalyst of creative liberation: an artistic and spiritual awakening of the divine spark against dogmatic religious constraints.

On July 3, 2001, Sovereign Patriarch Michael Bertiaux formally consecrated the Scandinavian leaders of the Neo-Luciferian Church (NLC) into the episcopate of the Ecclesia Gnostica Spiritualis. In this synodal act:

  • Tau Lucem Fero (Bjarne Salling Pedersen): Consecrated to the episcopate and elevated to the primatial office of Archbishop of the Neo-Luciferian Church.
  • Tau Philo Sophia (Isabel Munksgaard Berg): Consecrated to the episcopate as Bishop and co-founder of the Neo-Luciferian Church.
  • Tau Ogdoade-Orfeo IV (Michael Bertiaux): Formally assumed the title of Honorary Arch-Bishop of the Neo-Luciferian Church, serving as patron and primary lineage transmitter for the Scandinavian current.
